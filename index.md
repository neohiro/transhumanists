  <section id="about" aria-labelledby="about-heading">
    <h2 id="about-heading">About transhumanists</h2>
    <p>
      The transhumanists network explores the convergence of human biology, technology,
      and artificial intelligence. We document advances in longevity science, cognitive
      augmentation, biohacking, and AI-integrated cognition — from nootropics to neural
      interfaces, from cryonics to mind uploading.
    </p>
    <p>
      This is a knowledge hub for the intersection of human enhancement technologies,
      curated research, and open-source tooling for self-experimentation and biohacking.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section id="topics" aria-labelledby="topics-heading">
    <h2 id="topics-heading">Core Topics</h2>
    <div class="features-grid">
      <article class="feature">
        <h3>Longevity & Anti-Aging</h3>
        <p>Research into senolytics, rapamycin, NAD+ precursors, and computational approaches to understanding biological aging.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="feature">
        <h3>Cognitive Enhancement</h3>
        <p>Nootropics, neurofeedback, tDCS, and evidence-based approaches to improving memory, focus, and creativity.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="feature">
        <h3>Neural Interfaces</h3>
        <p>Brain-computer interfaces, neural implants, and the emerging field of mind-computer integration.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="feature">
        <h3>Biohacking</h3>
        <p>DIY biology, open-source lab equipment, quantified self, and community-driven health optimization.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="feature">
        <h3>AI-Human Integration</h3>
        <p>Intelligence amplification through AI tools, external cognition, and the ethics of enhanced cognition.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="feature">
        <h3>Open Research</h3>
        <p>Open-source science, preprint sharing, reproducibility, and community-driven research in longevity and biohacking.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </section>
